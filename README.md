## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 💳 1. UPI Transaction Analysis

- **Objective:** Analyse UPI digital payment activity to understand transaction volumes, account balances and spending patterns across time, cities, banks, merchants and customer segments.
- **Tools:** Power BI Service, Excel (data source), DAX, Power Query
- **Data Source:** `UPI+Transactions.xlsx`, connected through a gateway to a Power BI semantic model with scheduled refresh
- **Architecture:** Excel dataset → Power BI semantic model → Interactive report (2 pages)
- **Report Pages:**
  - **Page 1 – Monthly Trends:** Balance and transaction amount by month for 2024. Toggle buttons switch between line and column views for both amount and balance.
  - **Page 2 – City View:** Matrix of transaction amount and remaining balance by city, currency and month.
- **Interactive Filters:** Bank (sent/received), City, Device Type, Gender, Age Group, Merchant, Payment Method, Purpose and Transaction Type
- **Key Insights:**
  - Monthly balance stayed stable through 2024, at roughly 8.2M–8.5M, with a small peak in June.
  - Transaction amounts per city-month cluster around 1.6M–1.7M, with no single city dominating.
  - The filters allow drill-down by demographic, device and payment method to find the segments driving activity.
- **Business Value:** Helps banks and payment providers monitor payment trends, understand customer behaviour and target segments by city, age group or merchant type.
- **Project Link:** https://app.powerbi.com/links/GWNe0N7FoC?ctid=9c375136-2e2e-4665-b3e8-65fd4170c609&pbi_source=linkShare&bookmarkGuid=dfac6aeb-7558-4284-86f8-1a6a80b50d7b

### ☁️ 2. Azure Power BI Report – Men's Fashion Brand Analysis

- **Objective:** Analyse men's fashion brands across pricing, discounting, product variety and profitability, to show which brands perform best and where margins are weakest.
- **Tools:** Azure SQL Database, Power BI Service (semantic model and published report), DAX, Power BI Dataflows
- **Architecture:** Azure SQL Database → Power BI semantic model (scheduled refresh) → Interactive report (Brands and Details pages)
- **Report Pages:**
  - **Brands:** Landing page with a brand list for quick filtering and navigation.
  - **Details:** Dashboard of brand-level KPIs.
- **Key Visuals:**
  - Top 5 brands by average discount %
  - Top 5 brands by highest average profit %
  - Top 5 brands by number of varieties
  - Top 5 brands by highest average sales price
  - Bottom 5 brands by average profit %
- **Key Insights:**
  - The Indian Garage Co offers the deepest average discount (72%) and also has the widest range (51 varieties, ~23% of the top 5 total), which may put pressure on its margins.
  - Discount levels vary widely among the top 5 (72% down to 32%), which points to very different pricing strategies.
  - Premium brands such as Armani Exchange lead on average sales price, while the highest average profit % is shared by several brands at around 17%.
  - The bottom 5 brands by profit % sit well below the leaders and are candidates for pricing or discount review.
- **Business Value:** Helps merchandising and pricing teams balance discounts, product variety and profit across brands.
- **Project Link:** https://app.powerbi.com/links/CO9CJLdurt?ctid=9c375136-2e2e-4665-b3e8-65fd4170c609&pbi_source=linkShare&bookmarkGuid=b1338f0a-dc37-4df6-ba6e-a0cee33edbe2
